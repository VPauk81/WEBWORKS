    FAQ

    Good questions

    Can you build something that is not in the configurator?

    Yes. The configurator covers the most common builds; for anything else describe your task and we will prepare an individual quote.

    Do I get the source code?

    Always. The firmware source and a short guide are included, so you can change the device yourself later.

    How long does it take?

    Usually 3 to 7 working days. The exact time for your build is shown in the configurator.

    What if something does not work?

    Every device has a 12-month warranty. We repair or replace it and help you with setup free of charge.

    Which payment and delivery options are available?

    Bank transfer or card, with tracked delivery across Europe. The final price includes testing and packaging.

    Have a device in mind?

    Describe your idea and we will suggest the simplest way to build it. Answer within one working day.
