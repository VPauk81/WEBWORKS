FAQ

Good questions

Where do the messages go?

To your e-mail, a Google Sheet, your CRM — or all of them at once. You choose.

Will it add the form to my existing site?

Yes. The form can be embedded into any site or published as a separate page on your domain.

How is spam handled?

Hidden checks and rate limits block bots, so real visitors never have to solve puzzles.

Is customer data safe?

Data goes only where you send it. The form asks for GDPR consent and collects only what you need.

Can I change the fields?

Yes — fields, topics, texts and auto-reply are fully adjustable, in all site languages.
