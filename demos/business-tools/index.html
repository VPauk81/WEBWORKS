FAQ

Good questions

Do I need to know Google Sheets?

Only the basics — if you can edit a table, you can manage orders. We also show you how.

Are there monthly fees?

No fees for the tools themselves. Your own Google account and domain are free or cost a few euros a year.

Can customers pay online?

Yes, a payment link can be added to the confirmation e-mail or the order page.

Is customer data safe?

Data stays in your own Google account. The form asks for GDPR consent and collects only what the order needs.

Can I change the statuses?

Yes — names, order and e-mail texts are fully adjustable, in all site languages.
