FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Give us a call — our service advisor will help right away.

Will servicing here affect my manufacturer warranty?

No. Under EU rules you are free to service your car at an independent garage without losing the manufacturer's warranty. We follow the official service schedule, use approved oils and parts, and update your service book.

Can I bring my own parts?

Yes. We'll fit your parts, but in that case the warranty covers our labour only.

How long is the wait without an appointment?

Usually 20–40 minutes for simple jobs. To skip the wait, book online — it takes a minute.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash, debit and credit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, bank transfer, and invoices for business customers.

Do you have a waiting area?

Yes: coffee, Wi-Fi and a window into the workshop, so you can watch the work on your car.
