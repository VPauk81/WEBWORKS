    FAQ

    Good questions

    Can I change or cancel my booking?

    Yes. Just let us know at least 12 hours before the visit and we will move it free of charge.

    How long does gel polish last?

    Usually up to three weeks, depending on your nails and daily care. We give you care tips after every visit.

    Do you work with sensitive skin?

    Yes. Tell us about any allergies when you book and your master will choose gentle products for you.

    Can I bring a friend?

    Of course — book two neighbouring times or ask for a double visit and enjoy it together.

    What payment methods do you accept?

    Card, mobile payment and cash. Gift cards are welcome too.
